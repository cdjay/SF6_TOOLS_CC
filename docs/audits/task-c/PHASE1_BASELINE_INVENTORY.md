## 7. Phase-One Correctness Conclusions

Proven at this stage:

- The baseline SHA has 115 parseable tracked Lua files.
- All 59 discovered executable test files pass in the Task C worktree.
- The sealed 633-case files retain their pinned integrity contract.
- The 965-file frozen loose corpus is complete against the tracked manifest.
- All 31 characters exist in that frozen corpus, with severe distribution
  imbalance.

Not proven at this stage:

- Any production consumer is correct over the 965-case corpus.
- The sealed oracle is independent from the implementation or current data.
- Detection false-positive/false-negative behavior over near misses.
- Same-frame, side-switch, mirror, repeated roundtrip, or translation
  metamorphic invariants over real corpus.
- Recording/display/detection/demo/playback agreement over the same cases.
- Generator determinism over authoritative full inputs.
- Full fast-forward/catch-up timing behavior.
- Real-game correctness.
